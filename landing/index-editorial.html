TermLoop
APR 17 · 2026 · An agentic terminal IDE

A terminal
where agents
do the typing.

TermLoop is a macOS terminal IDE designed for running AI agents in parallel. Every task gets its own git worktree. A native iOS bridge lets you drive your agents from your phone. One terminal, many loops.

Status pre-alpha Platform macOS · iOS Built on cmux Launch 2026

§ First-principles Three tenets
01

Parallel agent sessions.

Run four Claudes, a Codex, and a Gemini at once — each in its own pane, each in its own worktree. Nothing collides. Nothing waits its turn.

multiplexed isolated lossless
02

Worktrees, first-class.

Every task gets its own branch and its own directory, created automatically. No stashing. No branch gymnastics. Agents can't step on work in progress.

zero-config git-native disposable
03

Phone in the loop.

Start an agent from your couch. Check output from a coffee-shop queue. A native iOS client speaks to TermLoop over an authenticated TCP bridge.

bridged encrypted async-native
